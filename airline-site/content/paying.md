## How long you have to pay {#deadline} {model: ONE-304}

Each item in your booking shows a payment deadline. We accept a payment made before that time, and we turn down one made at or after it. The amounts you pay must add up to the price of the items you are paying for. If a payment method carries a card fee, we charge it on top of the amount you pay with that method.

## Paying with more than one method {#several} {model: ONE-301}

You can split a payment across more than one card or account. The payment goes through only when every part succeeds. If any part is declined, the whole payment fails, and you can try again.

## When your booking counts as paid {#paid} {model: ONE-302}

An item counts as paid once a payment covering it has gone through. While a payment is still pending, or if it failed, the item is not paid yet and nothing in your trip changes.

## Updates from your bank {#updates} {model: ONE-303}

We record the result of each card or account as it reaches us. A result for one card leaves the other parts of the same payment as they were.

## If you do not pay in time {#unpaid} {model: ONE-306}

If the payment deadline passes and no payment has gone through, we remove the unpaid items from your booking. Any payment still pending for them is cancelled. An item that is paid is never removed this way.

## If you send your booking twice {#twice} {model: ONE-403}

If your connection drops and you send the same booking again, we check whether that offer has already been booked. If it has, you get the booking you already have, or we turn down the second request. Either way, we tell you.

## Still being decided {#deciding}

### A booking with no payment deadline {open: ONE-306.5}

Now and then an item is booked without a payment deadline. We have not yet decided how long an unpaid item like that can stay in your booking. If you see one, contact us and we will set a deadline with you.

### Booking twice from one offer {open: ONE-403.4}

We have not yet decided how we recognize a repeated booking in every case. If you find two bookings for the same trip, contact us and we will cancel the duplicate.
