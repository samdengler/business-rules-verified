## How we move you to new flights {#notice} {model: ONE-705}

If we move you to other flights, the new flights replace the old ones in your booking at the same price, for every traveler on the old flight. Some changes need your answer first. Until you answer, your booking stays as it was.

## Your options {#options} {model: ONE-701}

A change notice can offer you choices, such as accepting the new flights, looking for other flights, or cancelling. The notice stays open until you answer with one of them. If a notice offers no choices, we only ask you to confirm you have seen it.

## If you do not answer in time {#no-answer} {model: ONE-702}

A choice can come with a deadline. If you have not answered when the first deadline passes, we do what the notice says happens then. Until a deadline passes, nothing happens without your answer.

## Saying no to a change {#decline} {model: ONE-703}

If you cancel because of our change, we price it as a cancellation of the affected flights. If you choose other flights instead, we price it as a change to those flights.

## When we cancel your flight {#cancelled} {model: ONE-704}

If we cancel a flight and do not move you to another one, everything you booked on that flight is cancelled. Your other flights stay as they were, and your booking keeps its record of what you paid.

## Still being decided {#deciding}

### Money back when we cancel a flight {open: ONE-704.4}

We have not yet decided how much of your fare comes back when we cancel a flight and cannot move you. If this happens to you, contact us and we will quote your refund directly.

### Extras not yet confirmed when we cancel a flight {open: ONE-704.6}

Some extras on a flight are still being arranged when we cancel it, such as a request we have not confirmed yet, and some are already under way. We have not yet decided how those end. If you have one, contact us and we will cancel it with you.

### Fees when you decline our change {open: ONE-703.4, ONE-703.5}

We have not yet decided whether our cancellation and change fees apply when you turn down a change we made. If this is your case, contact us before you choose and we will tell you what it costs.
